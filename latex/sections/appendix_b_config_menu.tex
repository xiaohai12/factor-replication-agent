\section{Configuration Menu and Clamping Rules}
\label{app:config-menu}

The agent never writes portfolio-construction or backtest-control code. After
review, \texttt{registry.build\_config} resolves the MethodSpec into the
engine's fixed menu. Table~\ref{tab:config-menu} lists the principal
menu-governed choices. A paper value that is absent, unspecified, or outside
the implemented menu is deterministically replaced by the stated default and
recorded in the resolved configuration's \texttt{defaults\_applied} field.
This is a scope limitation, not an inference that the default reproduces the
paper's choice.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \small
  \caption{Principal fixed-menu controls used by the backtest engine.}
  \label{tab:config-menu}
  \begin{tabular}{p{0.25\linewidth}p{0.43\linewidth}p{0.22\linewidth}}
    \toprule
    Control & Implemented values & Default when unspecified or off-menu \\
    \midrule
    Breakpoint source & Full-sample universe; NYSE universe & NYSE \\
    Portfolio weighting & Value weighted; equal weighted & Value weighted \\
    Missing-signal action & Drop; unspecified & Drop \\
    Return combination & Extreme-group spread; average-leg spread;
      full-portfolio return; single-signal-portfolio return; unspecified &
      Extreme-group spread \\
    Rebalance frequency & Annual; quarterly; monthly & Monthly \\
    Number of target-sort groups & Integer of at least two & Ten groups \\
    Estimator & Portfolio sort & Portfolio sort \\
    Return basis & Excess return & Excess return \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The universe filters, sample window, formation month, accounting lag, and
portfolio legs are also resolved from the approved MethodSpec, subject to
schema validation and the data catalog. They are not free-text code generated
by the agent. Track-specific experimental overrides are stricter than paper
resolution: an unknown key or an override value outside a menu is rejected,
rather than silently clamped, because a no-op override would invalidate the
claimed configuration contrast. Unsupported sort modes or constructions fail
loudly rather than being reinterpreted by the engine.
